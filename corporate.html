CORPORATE EVENT BAND GTA

Professional live music that elevates your event

LIVE MUSIC THAT MAKES AN IMPRESSION

The Covernotes are a 4-piece professional cover band with extensive experience performing at corporate events across Toronto and the GTA. From intimate team dinners to large-scale company celebrations, we bring the energy and professionalism that corporate events demand.

We know that a corporate event is a reflection of your company. We show up on time, set up efficiently, dress the part and deliver a performance that gets people talking. Whether you need a sophisticated background atmosphere to start the evening or a full-energy dance set to close it out, we adapt our performance to suit your brief.

Our versatile set list covers pop, classic rock, motown, soul, R&B, latin, reggae and hip hop, giving us the range to entertain guests of all tastes and ages throughout the night.

PERFECT FOR EVERY CORPORATE OCCASION

Christmas Parties

Make your annual company Christmas party one people actually look forward to. High energy sets and festive classics that get the whole office dancing.

Gala Dinners

Sophisticated background music during dinner and a full live set to follow. We work to your schedule and venue to create the right atmosphere at every stage.

Rooftop and Patio Events

Experienced with outdoor and semi-outdoor corporate venues. We bring everything we need and work with your venue to make it seamless.

Team Celebrations

Company milestones, product launches, team away days and end-of-year parties. Live music turns a good event into a great one.

Fundraising Galas

We've performed at charity galas and fundraising events across the GTA and understand the importance of energy, timing and crowd management.

Fully Self-Contained

We bring our own professional PA system and equipment. Punctual, professionally dressed and easy to work with from first contact to last song.
