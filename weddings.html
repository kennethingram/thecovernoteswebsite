WEDDING BAND TORONTO & GTA

Live music that makes your day unforgettable

YOUR WEDDING DESERVES LIVE MUSIC

The Covernotes are a 4-piece live cover band based in Oakville, bringing energy, elegance and a packed dance floor to wedding receptions across Toronto, Oakville, Burlington, Mississauga, Hamilton and the wider GTA.

We understand that your wedding day is one of the most important days of your life. From the first dance to the last song of the night, we work closely with you to tailor our performance to your vision. Whether you want a sophisticated cocktail atmosphere to start and a high-energy party to finish or something completely bespoke, we make it happen.

With hundreds of performances under our belts and a set list spanning pop, classic rock, motown, soul, R&B, latin, reggae and hip hop, we have the experience and the repertoire to keep guests of all ages on the dance floor all night long.

WHAT'S INCLUDED

Full Live Performance

4-piece band with lead vocals, guitar, bass and drums. Multiple vocalists for a varied and dynamic sound throughout the evening.

Tailored Set List

We customise our set to suit your taste and the flow of your event. First dance requests and special song moments are always welcome.

3 Hours On Stage

Our standard package includes 3 hours on site with flexible set arrangements to fit your evening's running order.

Background Music

We provide playlist music during breaks as standard so the atmosphere stays consistent throughout your reception.

Professional Equipment

We bring our own PA system and equipment. No need to source a separate sound company for your venue.

GTA Wide Coverage

Based in Oakville and available across Toronto, Mississauga, Burlington, Hamilton and beyond. We travel across Ontario for the right event.
