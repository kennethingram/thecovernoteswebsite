ABOUT THE COVERNOTES

Ontario's live party and events band

WHO WE ARE

The Covernotes are a 4-piece live cover band based in Oakville, Ontario. With hundreds of performances across Canada and beyond, we bring experience, professionalism and genuine love for live music to every event we play.

We play everything from classics to current floor-fillers. Pop, classic rock, motown, soul, R&B, latin, reggae and hip hop — our set list covers every mood and decade and we tailor every performance to the event and the crowd in front of us.

We are multi-instrumentalists and vocalists with professional experience performing at venues of all sizes, from intimate private parties to large outdoor festivals with audiences of over a thousand people. We pride ourselves on reliability, punctuality and giving 100% every time.

We're fun and flexible with a professional attitude. Easy to communicate with in the lead-up to your event and focused on making the night everything you want it to be.

WHAT SETS US APART

Experience

Hundreds of performances across Canada and internationally. We've played weddings, corporate events, outdoor festivals, rooftop parties, street parties and everything in between.

Versatility

100+ songs spanning multiple genres and decades. We read the room and adapt our set in real time to keep the energy exactly where it needs to be.

Professionalism

We show up on time, set up efficiently and communicate clearly from first enquiry through to the last song. No drama, no fuss.

Fully Self-Contained

We bring our own professional PA system and equipment. No need to source a separate sound company or coordinate with third parties on your behalf.

Personal Touch

We take song requests seriously and love making events personal. Whether it's a first dance, a special dedication or a must-play track, we'll make it happen.

GTA Based

Based in Oakville and available across Toronto, Mississauga, Burlington, Hamilton and the wider GTA. We also travel across Ontario for the right event.

EVENTS WE PLAY

We specialise in weddings, corporate events and private parties but our experience spans a much wider range of occasions. We've performed at street parties, outdoor festivals, fundraising galas, community events, rooftop parties, birthday celebrations, anniversaries, holiday parties and more.

Whatever the event, the same commitment applies: turn up, perform brilliantly and make sure your guests have a night they won't forget.
