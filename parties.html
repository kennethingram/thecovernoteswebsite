THE LIFE OF YOUR PARTY

The Covernotes are a 4-piece live cover band based in Oakville, available to hire for private parties across Toronto, Mississauga, Burlington, Hamilton and the wider GTA. From milestone birthdays to anniversary celebrations and everything in between, we bring the energy that turns a good night into a great one.

We're experienced crowd readers. We know when to dial it up and when to let the room breathe. Our set list of 100+ songs spans pop, classic rock, motown, soul, R&B, latin, reggae and hip hop, giving us the range to keep guests of all ages and all tastes on the dance floor all night long.

We're easy to work with, flexible with timings and take requests seriously. Tell us your must-play songs and we'll make them happen.

EVERY OCCASION COVERED

Birthday Parties

Whether it's a 30th, 40th, 50th or beyond, a live band makes a milestone birthday something people will talk about for years. We take requests and personalise the night for the guest of honour.

Anniversary Parties

From silver to golden anniversaries, we set the tone for a celebration that feels as special as the occasion deserves. First song dedications always welcome.

House and Garden Parties

We've played intimate house parties and large garden events alike. We'll talk through your space and make sure the setup works perfectly for your venue.

Fundraisers and Galas

High energy and crowd-pleasing sets that suit charity evenings, community fundraisers and gala events across the GTA.

Street and Outdoor Events

Experienced performing at outdoor events and street parties. We bring our own PA and are comfortable in a wide range of outdoor setups.

Song Requests Welcome

Give us your must-play list in advance and we'll incorporate your favourite tracks into the set. We love making the night personal for you and your guests.
